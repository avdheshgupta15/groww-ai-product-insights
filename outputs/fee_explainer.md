# Understanding Your Charges: DP & External Fees

We've noticed some confusion regarding the charges applied to your account during stock sales. We want to make these costs transparent.

## What are DP Charges?
**DP (Depository Participant) Charges** are flat fees charged by the depository (CDSL or NSDL) and the broker whenever you sell shares from your demat account. 

**Common Confusion:** *"I already paid for CDSL, why is Groww charging me again?"*
DP charges are not a recurring monthly subscription. They are **transaction-based**. Every time you sell a stock (regardless of the quantity), a DP charge is applied. This is a standard industry practice across all Indian brokers.

## Why is my final amount lower than the sale price?
When you sell a stock, the final amount credited to your wallet is:
`Sale Value - (Brokerage + DP Charges + STT + GST + SEBI Charges)`

**Example:**
If you sell 1 share of a company for ₹35.88, and the total of these regulatory and broker charges is ₹6.19, you will receive ₹29.69.

## Summary Table
| Charge Type | When is it applied? | Who collects it? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brokerage** | Per trade (Buy/Sell) | Groww |
| **DP Charges** | Per stock sold (per day) | Depository / Broker |
| **STT** | On every transaction | Government |
| **GST** | On Brokerage & DP charges | Government |

For a detailed breakdown of your specific trade, please visit the **Reports** section in the app and download your **Contract Note**.
