# Weekly Product Pulse: Groww User Feedback

**Date:** October 2026
**Data Source:** 31 Public Reviews (Google Play, App Store, Reddit, Twitter)

## Executive Summary
The majority of user frustration stems from technical instability during critical trading moments and a perceived lack of transparency/speed in the redemption process. While the UI is praised as beginner-friendly, the underlying performance is failing experienced traders.

## Key Recurring Themes

### 1. App Stability & Performance
- **Observation:** Frequent reports of the app "hanging," "freezing," or "lagging," especially during peak market volatility.
- **Impact:** Users are unable to exit positions or place orders, leading to financial losses.
- **Representative Quote:** *"Your app gets stuck far too often during trade execution, which caused me a loss in my trade."*

### 2. Redemption & Withdrawal Delays
- **Observation:** A significant number of users report that redeeming money takes 5-7 working days, often with poor support responses.
- **Impact:** Erosion of trust in the platform's liquidity and reliability.
- **Representative Quote:** *"Whenever I want to redeem money, processing speed is slow. Takes around five days."*

### 3. Order Execution Reliability
- **Observation:** Reports of "stuck" orders or orders not executing automatically despite stop-loss/target settings.
- **Impact:** High frustration among active traders.
- **Representative Quote:** *"even after setting a profit/loss limit, the order does not get executed automatically."*

## Priority Action Items
1. **Performance Sprint:** Optimize app response time and stability during high-volatility windows.
2. **Redemption Transparency:** Implement a real-time tracking system for redemptions to reduce support tickets.
3. **Execution Audit:** Review the order execution engine to ensure stop-loss/target triggers are reliable.
